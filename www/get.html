Get Sprout on your phone

Point your phone's camera at the code for your phone.

🍎 iPhone

  1. Open the Camera app and point it at the code. Tap the link that pops up.
  2. Sprout opens in Safari. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Sprout from your Home Screen once while online, so it saves everything for offline.
Or type: https://md505d1-art.github.io/New-App.py/

🤖 Android

  1. Open the Camera (or Google Lens) and point it at the code. Tap the link.
  2. Sprout.apk downloads. Open it from your notifications or Downloads.
  3. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then tap Install.
Or go to: https://github.com/md505d1-art/New-App.py/releases/download/apk-latest/Sprout.apk

Your progress is saved on the phone itself. Sprout works offline once it's installed.
